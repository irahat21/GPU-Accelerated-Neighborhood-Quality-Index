About NYC Quality Index

The NYC Quality Index is a comprehensive tool designed to help New Yorkers make informed decisions about where to live, work, and invest. By aggregating and visualizing key quality-of-life metrics across all NYC zip codes, we provide transparent, data-driven insights into neighborhood livability.

Our Mission

We believe everyone deserves access to clear, accurate information about their neighborhood's quality. Our platform democratizes data that was previously scattered across multiple city agencies, making it easy to understand and compare neighborhoods at a glance.

Methodology

Our quality scores are calculated using a transparent, data-driven approach. Each metric is scored on a scale of 0-100, with higher scores indicating better conditions.

Air Quality Index (AQI)

Based on EPA air quality monitoring data, measuring particulate matter (PM2.5, PM10), ozone levels, and other pollutants. Scores are normalized to a 0-100 scale where:

  • 90-100: Excellent - Minimal health concerns
  • 70-89: Good - Acceptable air quality
  • 50-69: Moderate - Sensitive groups may be affected
  • 30-49: Poor - Health concerns for general population
  • 0-29: Very Poor - Serious health risks

Drinking Water Safety

Combines NYC DEP water quality reports, lead testing results, and infrastructure age. Factors include:

  • Bacterial contamination levels
  • Lead and heavy metal concentrations
  • Chlorine and disinfection byproducts
  • Water main age and replacement rates
  • Complaint frequency and resolution

Education Quality

Aggregates NYC Department of Education data including:

  • Average standardized test scores (Math & ELA)
  • High school graduation rates
  • College readiness indicators
  • Student-teacher ratios
  • School funding per student
  • Advanced course availability

NYPD Complaint Index

Derived from NYPD complaint data, measuring public safety through:

  • Total complaint volume per 1,000 residents
  • Complaint type severity weighting
  • Response time averages
  • Resolution rates
  • Year-over-year trend analysis

Note: Higher scores indicate fewer complaints and better public safety conditions.

Overall Score Calculation

Overall Score = (Air Quality + Water Safety + Education + NYPD) / 4

The overall score provides a holistic view of neighborhood quality, giving equal weight to each metric. Users can also view and filter by individual metrics to prioritize what matters most to them.

Data Sources

All data is sourced from official New York City agencies and public datasets:

  • Air Quality: EPA AirNow & NYC Department of Health
  • Water Safety: NYC Department of Environmental Protection (DEP)
  • Education: NYC Department of Education Open Data
  • Public Safety: NYPD Complaint Data (NYC Open Data)
